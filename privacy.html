PHYSICS HANDBOOK

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Physics Handbook is an educational physics reference published by Oleg Korunov. This policy covers the mobile app on iOS and Android, including optional Physics Pro features introduced on iOS in version 1.1.

No accounts or tracking

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. We do not operate a server that receives your questions, photos, searches or study activity.

What stays on your device

The app stores your chosen language, theme and favourite articles locally. On iOS it also caches the verified Pro entitlement and subscription expiry to manage access. Calculator inputs, searches and tutor conversations stay in memory while you use the app. Conversations are not saved as a history and are lost when the app process ends.

The reference works offline

Articles, worked examples, translations and fonts are included in the app. Search, formula calculators, the cheat sheet, constants and unit tables work offline and remain free.

Apple Intelligence tutor

The optional iOS tutor uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework on an eligible device with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later and Apple Intelligence enabled. Questions and answers are processed on the device; the app does not send them to us or a cloud AI provider. For languages not directly supported by the model, the app can use Apple's on-device Translation framework. The system may need an internet connection to download its models first. AI answers can contain errors and should be checked against the reference.

Camera and selected photos

The optional iOS problem scanner opens the camera only at your request, with system permission. Photos are recognized locally using Apple's Vision framework and are not saved by the app. The photo picker gives the app only the photo you choose. You can review and edit recognized text before asking the tutor. The app does not request microphone, contacts or location access. The Android app has no tutor, scanner or in-app purchases.

Purchases and the operating system

Physics Pro offers monthly and yearly subscriptions and a lifetime non-consumable purchase through the App Store. Apple processes payments; we never receive card details. The app verifies transactions using StoreKit and stores only local access status. App stores and the operating system handle installation, updates, purchases and any system diagnostics under their own policies. Purchases, restoration and model downloads may require internet access.

Deleting local information

Start a new conversation to clear the current tutor conversation. To delete saved settings, clear the app's data or uninstall it. iOS preferences may be included in system-managed device backups. Android settings and favourites are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription: manage subscriptions in your App Store account. Eligible purchases can be restored through the App Store.

Children

The app is a study aid and does not collect personal information or show advertising. Parents should manage purchase permissions through their device's parental controls.
