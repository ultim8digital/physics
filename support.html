How can we help?

Find a formula

Search accepts the name of a law, a formula, a quantity or a symbol. The chips above the list filter by topic, by your starred articles, or show only articles that have a calculator.

Use a calculator

Open an article and choose the value you want to find. Enter the values you know in the units shown next to each field — all quantities are in SI units, and angles are entered in degrees. The app solves the formula shown in the article for the value you chose; the reasoning for a full solution is in the worked example below it.

Build a cheat sheet

Star an article to add it to your favourites, then open the cheat sheet from the toolbar and filter it by favourites or by topic. It lists formulas without the explanations, for quick revision.

Change the language

Open the overflow menu, then About. The language menu is above the licences. Version 1.1 supports 20 languages: English, Spanish, Arabic, French, Hindi, Thai, Russian, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian Bokmål, Turkish, Vietnamese, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. The reference is available offline. The app follows a supported device language on first launch and remembers your manual choice.

Constants, units and how to solve problems

The overflow menu holds the table of physical constants, the SI units with their prefixes, and a short problem-solving guide.

Physics Pro on iPhone and iPad

Version 1.1 adds an optional on-device AI tutor and problem scanner. All reference articles, examples, calculators and the cheat sheet remain free. Pro is available through the App Store as monthly or yearly access, with a seven-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, or a lifetime purchase. Prices and eligibility appear on the purchase screen. Subscriptions renew automatically; manage or cancel them in App Store settings.

Apple Intelligence availability

The tutor requires an eligible device running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, Apple Intelligence enabled, and a downloaded model. Language and regional availability depend on Apple and your system. If a language needs on-device translation, the app offers the system download screen. Model downloads need a network connection. AI answers can contain mistakes; check formulas, units and results against the reference.

Scan a problem

Open Tutor, then choose the camera or a photo. Text recognition happens on the device. Review the recognized text, especially equations, subscripts and units, before asking the tutor. If Apple Intelligence is unavailable, Pro can still recognize and copy text from a selected photo. The Android app has no Pro purchases, tutor or scanner.

Restore a purchase

Use Restore purchases on the Pro screen with the same App Store account. An active subscription or verified lifetime purchase unlocks Pro. A subscription stops providing access when it expires. Deleting the app does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Report a problem or a correction

Email ultim8digital@gmail.com with the app version (shown in About), your device and operating-system version, and what you did before the problem appeared. For a physics or translation correction, name the article and the exact line, and add a reliable reference if you have one. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

About the content

The reference follows standard school and introductory university treatments. Each article states the conditions under which its formula holds; outside them it gives a wrong answer. Translations include on-device machine-assisted translations and continue to be refined. Corrections are welcome.
