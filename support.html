Find a formula

Search accepts the name of a law, a formula, a quantity or a symbol. The chips above the list filter by topic, by your starred articles, or show only articles that have a calculator.

Use a calculator

Open an article and choose the value you want to find. Enter the values you know in the units shown next to each field, including the angle unit where applicable. Check the conditions stated in the article before applying its formula. The app solves the displayed formula for the value you chose; the reasoning for a full solution is in the worked example below it.

Build a cheat sheet

Star an article to add it to your favourites, then open the cheat sheet from the toolbar and filter it by favourites or by topic. It lists formulas without the explanations, for quick revision.

Change the language

Open the overflow menu, then About. Choose a language from the menu above the licences. Install the latest app update to get the latest language resources. The reference is available offline. The app follows a supported device language on first launch and remembers your manual choice. Tutor language availability is separate from the reference language menu; see Apple Intelligence availability below.

Constants, units and how to solve problems

The overflow menu holds the table of physical constants, the SI units with their prefixes, and a short problem-solving guide.

Physics Pro on iPhone and iPad

Physics Pro offers an optional on-device AI tutor and problem scanner. All reference articles, examples, calculators and the cheat sheet remain free. Pro is available through the App Store as monthly or yearly access, with a seven-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, or a lifetime purchase. Prices and eligibility appear on the purchase screen. Subscriptions renew automatically; manage or cancel them in App Store settings.

Apple Intelligence availability

The tutor requires an eligible device running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, Apple Intelligence enabled, and a downloaded model. Language and regional availability depend on Apple and your system. If a language needs on-device translation, the app offers the system download screen. Model downloads need a network connection. AI answers can contain mistakes; check formulas, units and results against the reference.

Scan a problem

Open Tutor, then choose the camera or a photo. Text recognition happens on the device. Review the recognized text, especially equations, subscripts and units, before asking the tutor. If Apple Intelligence is unavailable, Pro can still recognize and copy text from a selected photo.

Restore a purchase

Use Restore purchases on the Pro screen with the same App Store account. An active subscription or verified lifetime purchase unlocks Pro. A subscription stops providing access when it expires. Deleting the app does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Report a problem or a correction

Email ultim8digital@gmail.com with the app version (shown in About), your device and operating-system version, and what you did before the problem appeared. For a physics or translation correction, name the article and the exact line, and add a reliable reference if you have one. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

About the content

The reference follows standard school and introductory university treatments. Check each article's stated conditions and units before using its formula. Localized reference content is bundled with the app and is available offline. Physics and translation corrections are welcome.

Вопросы

Работает без интернета?

Да. Справочник и его материалы доступны офлайн после установки. Для возможностей Apple Intelligence также нужны совместимое устройство и загруженная системная модель.

Что доступно бесплатно?

Приложение можно скачать бесплатно. Справочник остаётся полезным без Pro. Дополнительные инструменты и ИИ зависят от плана Pro конкретного приложения. Актуальные варианты указаны в App Store и на экране покупки.

Обязательно нужен Apple Intelligence?

Нет. Читать и пользоваться справочником можно без него. ИИ-репетитор — дополнительная возможность для совместимых iPhone и iPad с включённым Apple Intelligence. Поддержка языков зависит от системы.

На каких устройствах работает?

Приложение доступно для iPhone и iPad. Актуальные требования к устройству и версии системы указаны на странице App Store.

معلومات مفيدة

هل يعمل هذا التطبيق بدون اتصال بالإنترنت؟

نعم. يتم توفير "الدليل" ومحتوى المرجعية الخاص به بعد التثبيت، حتى في وضع عدم الاتصال. تتطلب ميزات Apple Intelligence أيضًا جهازًا متوافقًا وتنزيل نموذج نظام.

ما الذي يتوفر مجانًا؟

يمكنك تنزيل التطبيق مجانًا. يظل "الدليل" المرجعي مفيدًا بدون اشتراك Pro؛ تعتمد الأدوات التعليمية الإضافية وميزات الذكاء الاصطناعي على خطة Pro الخاصة بالتطبيق. تعرض صفحة متجر التطبيقات وشاشة الشراء داخل التطبيق الخيارات المتاحة حاليًا.

هل أحتاج إلى Apple Intelligence؟

لا، يمكنك قراءة واستخدام الكتيب دون الحاجة إليه. برنامج التدريس بالذكاء الاصطناعي اختياري ويتطلب جهاز iPhone أو iPad متوافق مع تقنية Apple Intelligence؛ وتعتمد اللغات المدعومة على نظام التشغيل الخاص بك.

ما هي الأجهزة التي يمكنني استخدامها؟

التطبيق متوفر لجهاز iPhone و iPad. يرجى التحقق من صفحة متجر التطبيقات للحصول على معلومات حول نظام التشغيل والأجهزة المطلوبة حاليًا.
